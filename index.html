One Million Years Trade Depot

Look up any No Man's Sky star system from its portal address.

Everything is worked out in your browser from the address alone, the same way the game generates it, down to the seeds of the system's ships and whether its exotic is a squid.

12 hex digits, or X:Y:Z:system like 064A:0082:01B9:009A.
