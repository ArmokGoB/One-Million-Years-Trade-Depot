One Million Years Trade Depot

Look up any No Man's Sky star system from its portal address.

Everything is worked out in your browser from the address alone, the same way the game generates it, down to the seeds of the system's ships. What those ships look like, and multi-tools, are next.

12 hex digits, or X:Y:Z:system like 025B:0082:03FF:004A.

0 is Euclid.

Fan-made and not affiliated with Hello Games. System data comes from a TypeScript port of nms_namegen by Stuart Coyle, had.sh and GoodGuysFree (MIT). Ship lists come from this project's own model of the game's generator, built on it and checked against systems recorded in game.
